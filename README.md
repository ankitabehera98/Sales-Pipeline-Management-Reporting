## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Organize and maintain sales opportunity data.
- Track deals across different pipeline stages.
- Monitor deal values and sales progress.
- Keep track of follow-up activities and opportunity status.
- Improve visibility into the overall sales pipeline.
- Create structured reports to support sales decisions.
