# Sales Pipeline Management & Reporting

## Project Overview
This project focuses on organizing and tracking sales opportunities through different stages of the sales pipeline. It demonstrates how Excel can be used to maintain sales records, monitor deal progress, track follow-ups, and create reports that support better sales management.

## Project Objectives
- Organize and maintain sales opportunity data.
- Track deals across different pipeline stages.
- Monitor deal values and sales progress.
- Keep track of follow-up activities and opportunity status.
- Improve visibility into the overall sales pipeline.
- Create structured reports to support sales decisions.

## Sales Pipeline Management Matrix

| Area | What Was Tracked | Business Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Lead Management | Prospect and lead details | Maintain organized prospect records |
| Opportunity Tracking | Deal details and sales stages | Monitor ongoing opportunities |
| Deal Management | Deal value and status | Understand potential revenue |
| Follow-up Tracking | Follow-up activities | Reduce missed opportunities |
| Pipeline Monitoring | Open and closed opportunities | Maintain pipeline visibility |
| Sales Reporting | Sales activity and pipeline summaries | Support performance reviews |

## Methodology
1. Organized sales-related information in an Excel workbook.
2. Structured opportunities based on their pipeline stages.
3. Tracked deal status, values, and follow-up activities.
4. Reviewed pipeline information to understand opportunity progress.
5. Used reporting features to present sales information in a structured format.
